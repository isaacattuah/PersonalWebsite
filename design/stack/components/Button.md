# Button

A pill-shaped link styled as a button, in two weights.

- **Primary**: `gold` fill with `gold-ink` text. The single most important action in view. One per view.
- **Quiet**: 2px `ink` outline on the ground. The alternative action beside a primary.
- On hover both fill with `ink` and switch text to `ground`. No movement, no shadow.
- Height 48px, `radius-pill`, `ui` type at weight 600.
- The label says what happens: "Read my writing", "Get in touch". No arrows.

You provide: the label and the destination.
