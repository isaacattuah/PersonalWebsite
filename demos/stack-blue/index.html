Selected systems and civic work

Things I designed, led or built.

  • Customer Scenario Simulator II

    Designer and builder

    A practice tool for Customer Engineers. One agent plays a customer with a hidden objective you have to uncover through discovery. A second agent grades the conversation against a rubric, with evidence for every score, and BigQuery tracks how your skills move over time.

    Claude on Vertex AI, FastAPI, Cloud Run, BigQuery. Code on GitHub. How it works.

  • Internal Agentic Compliance Assistant

    Product and tech lead, team of two engineers

    A workflow agent that lives in Google Chat and keeps five regional pods on top of a recurring compliance process. Pods reached 98 to 100 percent compliance, the team got back roughly 900 hours a year, and the project was sponsored for an org-wide pilot.

    Gemini, Apps Script, Google Chat.

  • Google.org civic AI fellowships

    AI Coach, 2026. 20% Software Engineer, 2024

    In 2026 I coached in the Google.org Accelerator: AI for Government Innovation, advising Code for America and co-leading the design sprint at the D.C. kickoff. In 2024 I spent a fifth of my time building a generative AI business-licensing chatbot and web portal for the 3,000+ businesses of Macon-Bibb County, Georgia.

    Gemini, Google Cloud.

  • Terraform CI/CD Pipeline Kit

    Author

    Terraform, Cloud Build, GKE, Cloud Run. Code on GitHub.
